Poulpix UI Immersive replaces Blizzard’s interface with a minimal HUD: thin bars, information at the edges of the screen, almost no frames. The world stays in the foreground. The HUD shows up when you need it (combat, damage taken, spell cast, low health) and fades away while you explore, walk around or rest in town.

![alt text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Poulpiix/media/refs/heads/main/PoulpixUIImmersive.webp)

## The HUD

*   Player: thin health and resource bars, class emblem, combo points, cast bar. Each element has its own shape: gradient, flat, brush strokes, orbs…
*   Target and focus: name coloured by reaction or class, level, guild, health bar, cast bar, buffs and debuffs. Rare, elite and boss borders, with the game’s dragon or skull beside the bar.
*   Distance to your target and your focus beside their frames, placed where you want.
*   Target of target, pet, totems, raid group badge as optional elements.
*   Your buffs and debuffs in their own elements, with several styles and icon shapes, weapon enchants included.
*   Smooth bars: bars slide to their new value and a trail shows what was just lost. Absorb shields and incoming heals on the health bars.
*   Cast bars with their own shapes, a colour for spells that can’t be interrupted, ticks on channelled spells and a latency zone.
*   Health and resource texts in the format of your choice, set separately for each kind of unit.

### Combat tools

*   Threat list of everyone on your target with the line not to cross, and your threat % beside the target and focus.
*   Swing timer, five second rule and mana ticks, the druid’s mana in cat or bear form, hunter pet mood.
*   Interrupt: cast bars coloured by whether your interrupt is ready, and an interrupt bar for your focus or target.
*   Combat text above your character, alerts in the middle of the screen (low health, low mana, aggro), movement spells with their cooldown, a crosshair.
*   Class reminders when a buff, pet, poison, stance or aura is missing; new spell ranks put on your bars for you.

### Action bars and cooldowns

*   Redrawn secure action bars, with 7 button shapes (square, brush square, round, octagon…), short hotkeys, range and mana tint. The game’s bars 6, 7 and 8 can be shown too.
*   Key binding mode: hover a button, press a key.
*   The game’s cooldown manager in the addon’s look: icons, column or bars, with layout presets.

### Nameplates

*   Nameplates in the HUD style for players, NPCs, monsters and pets: health bar, resource, class icon, cast bar, buffs and debuffs.

### Group and raid

*   The game’s group and raid frames, restyled: bar shapes, aura placement and shapes, health text, selected member highlighted.
*   Click casting: a spell on each mouse button (with Shift, Ctrl or Alt), and keys that cast on the member under the mouse.
*   Missing buffs: an icon on each member who lacks a buff you can cast.

### Bags and bank

*   The addon’s own bags: all your bags in one window, items sorted into categories or in a list, search, sorting, item level, quality borders.
*   Bank in the same style, your equipped bags and bank bag slots, the bags and gold of all your characters.
*   Quick actions to disenchant or pick a lock in one click, junk sold at vendors, a junk items window to clean your bags.

### Tools

*   Data bar: gold, durability, frame rate, latency, zone, reputation, professions, free slots, experience and time on one line.
*   Loot feed with gold per hour.
*   Automations: repair, sell junk, fast looting, cinematics skipped, “Train all” button, clams and boxes opened, companion pet summoned again…
*   One-key fishing, the skill needed in herb and ore tooltips, shorter stat lines on items, the best quest reward marked with a coin.

### Quests

*   Clean quest tracker with profession recipes, sorted by level, zone, progress or name, objectives coloured by progress.
*   Sounds when an objective or a quest is done, progress told to your group, quests accepted and turned in for you.

### World map and travel

*   Waypoint: a discreet diamond on screen with distance and arrival time. Ctrl + click on the map or your tracked quest.
*   Flight timer that learns how long each flight lasts.
*   Zone levels on the continent maps, flight masters and dungeon entrances on every map, coordinates, map fading while you move.

### Blizzard windows, restyled

*   Brush-painted background (13 styles) or flat dark background, set window by window, with your class colour as the accent.
*   Three-column character sheet: equipment, model and every stat at a glance.
*   Professions, quest log and map, contacts, guild, calendar, game options, loot rolls, tooltips (with a brush-stroke health bar)…
*   The addon’s own spellbook in the old layout and a macro editor, if you want them instead of the game’s.
*   Chat kept after a reload, with tabs in the addon’s look. The game’s damage meter in the theme, without touching its data.
*   Movable windows, positions remembered.

### World and immersion

*   Situational camera: over the shoulder, adapting to what you do (combat, mounted, indoors, close-up NPC dialogue).
*   Location reveal: the zone name appears in spaced capitals between two golden lines.
*   Animated effects from the game on any element, triggered by conditions (low health, aggro, missing buff, spell ready, critical hit…).
*   Vignette, red pulse at low health.
*   Chat, minimap and damage meter fade out with the HUD.

### Customisation

*   Unlock and move mode: move any element with the mouse or keyboard, on a grid. There is also pixel-precise positioning, layers and quick placements.
*   Simulation mode: animated sample data so you can set everything up without being in combat, including groups and raids of 5, 10, 20 and 40.
*   Named profiles per character, chosen automatically by situation if you want, with export and import.
*   Full settings window with search, organised by category.
*   Languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Chinese, Korean, Russian, Japanese

### Compatibility

*   Built for WoW Forever (Classic client 1.60.1).
*   No libraries required. Works with Questie, TomTom, Baganator and other addons’ minimap buttons.
